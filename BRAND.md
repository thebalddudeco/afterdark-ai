# Shadowframe AI brand system

Shadowframe's identity is a faceted shadow crystal holding a luminous spiral in motion. The crystal represents private, local control; the spiral represents transformation, image generation, and motion.

Every master SVG in this system is built from editable vector paths, polygons, gradients, masks, and filters. The artwork contains no embedded raster images. The SHADOWFRAME AI lettering is converted to outlined paths, so the lockups do not depend on an installed font.

## Logo rules

- Use the horizontal lockup whenever sufficient width is available.
- Use the stacked lockup for square and portrait placements.
- Use the crystal mark alone for app icons, favicons, avatars, and spaces narrower than 220 px.
- Keep clear space around the crystal equal to one quarter of its width.
- Place the orange lockups on black, graphite, photography, or very dark gradients.
- Do not stretch, rotate, redraw, recolor, or place effects over the crystal or wordmark.

## Editable vector masters

The complete masters are in `release/Shadowframe-Brand-Kit-v2/vector/`:

- `crystal-mark.svg`: transparent crystal and spiral symbol
- `app-icon.svg`: crystal on its app-icon background
- `logo-horizontal.svg`: horizontal outlined wordmark lockup
- `logo-stacked.svg`: portrait outlined wordmark lockup
- `banner-wide.svg`: 4000 × 1200 wide composition
- `social-card.svg`: 4000 × 2100 social composition
- `rune-pattern.svg`: seamless-style 4096-unit vector field

## Production derivatives

`public/brand/` contains web-ready SVG, PNG, JPG, and GIF derivatives, including social cards, GitHub artwork, app icons, favicons, manifest icons, and the hero background. `desktop/Shadowframe.Launcher/Assets/` contains the Windows PNG and multi-resolution ICO used by `Shadowframe.exe`.
